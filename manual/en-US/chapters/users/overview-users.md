## Users

Customer accounts combine Joomla access with redSHOP customer, address and shopper-group information. Handle personal data and account changes according to the shop's policies.

- [User management](chapters/users/user-management.md): find and manage customers.
- [Add or edit a customer](chapters/users/add-user.md): account, billing and registration guidance.
- [Shopper groups](chapters/users/shopper-groups.md): configure shop behavior for groups of customers.

## Manage customer information

1. Review the intended shopper group and Joomla permissions before creating or editing a customer.
2. Enter account and billing details, then save and reopen the record to check them. Existing customers can also have shipping addresses.
3. Use disposable accounts to verify registration, address access, group pricing and checkout before changing the live workflow.

Deleting a customer can also delete the associated Joomla user. Review permissions, order retention and privacy obligations, and take a backup before deletion. Test synchronization separately before using it on live records.
