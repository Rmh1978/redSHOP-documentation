## redSHOP Discount module

The optional `mod_redshop_discount` site module displays configured discounts. [Install and configure the module](chapters/module-redshop/install-module-plugin.md), assign its position and pages, and configure the relevant redSHOP discounts and shopper groups.

The inspected helper selects published discounts whose start and end dates include the current time and whose shopper-group association matches the visitor. The module has a class suffix and alternate-layout option. Test with disposable accounts from different shopper groups, before and during the date window, and check the cart totals separately: a displayed promotion does not itself establish that a discount will be applied.
