## VAT/Tax & Currency

Configure tax and currency settings for the shop and independently verify the resulting amounts:

- [VAT/Tax groups](chapters/vat/vat-group.md) organize rates and can be assigned to products.
- [VAT/Tax rates](chapters/vat/vat-rate.md) associate rates with groups, shopper groups and locations.
- [Currency](chapters/vat/currency.md) covers currency records, display settings and conversion precautions.

Tax obligations depend on your circumstances and jurisdiction. Consult a qualified adviser and test representative transactions on an isolated copy before applying changes.

## Plan and verify a change

1. Review the existing tax groups, rates and default group before creating or changing records. Associate rates with the appropriate groups, locations and shopper groups.
2. Check product tax-group assignments and the shop's price and currency configuration. A currency symbol or record alone does not establish an exchange rate.
3. Independently calculate representative results and compare product, cart, checkout and saved-order totals, including shipping, discounts and rounding.
4. Test guests and registered customers, relevant destinations and nonmatching cases with disposable data and mocked payments before changing a live shop.
