## e-conomic integration

The optional integration connects shop configuration, accounting mappings and product synchronization. Verify provider compatibility and accounting outcomes in an approved test environment.

The e-conomic plugin is an optional Marketplace integration. Its settings offer e-conomic and Reviso account types with different connection credentials; protect those credentials and confirm current provider support before enabling it.
