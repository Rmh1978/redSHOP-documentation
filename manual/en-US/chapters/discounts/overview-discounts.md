## Discounts

Open **Discount** in the redSHOP administrator to manage the available discount types. Choose the feature that matches your campaign:

- [Gift Cards](chapters/discounts/gift-cards.md) define gift-card products and issuance.
- [Mass Discount](chapters/discounts/mass-discount.md) updates discount fields on selected products.
- [Product Price Discount](chapters/discounts/product-price-discount.md) defines product-price rules for shopper groups.
- [Coupon](chapters/discounts/coupon.md) provides codes that customers can apply to a cart.
- [Order Total Discount](chapters/discounts/order-total-discount.md) defines subtotal-based rules for shopper groups.
- [Voucher](chapters/discounts/voucher.md) manages voucher codes and their restrictions.
- [Promotions](chapters/discounts/promotions.md) use promotion plugins for conditional awards.

These features can interact. Check each rule and its known limitations on an isolated shop with disposable data and mock payments before enabling a campaign.
