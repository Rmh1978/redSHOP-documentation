## Set up a product

1. Prepare a category and a suitable product template. Add a manufacturer or supplier if the product needs one.
2. Create the product with its name, product number, category and template. Review publication, pricing and tax settings before saving.
3. Reopen the product to check its saved settings. Add images or other media as needed, and configure product-specific options such as attributes and stock.
4. Preview the active storefront template and verify price, availability and checkout with disposable data before offering the product for sale.

Product media and protected downloads are separate workflows. See the product guide for Product Files and the media guide for gallery and deletion precautions. Price rules and bulk price changes also have known limitations; review the price guide before applying them.
