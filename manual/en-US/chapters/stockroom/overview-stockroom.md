## Stockroom

Stockrooms organize inventory and can supply delivery or availability information. Review the stockroom configuration and use disposable data to test how your templates and checkout use stock.

- [Stockroom management](chapters/stockroom/stockroom-management.md): define stockrooms and delivery settings.
- [Inventory for all stockrooms](chapters/stockroom/inventory-stockroom.md): view and update stock and preorder quantities.
- [Stockroom image management](chapters/stockroom/stockroom-image-management.md): configure stock images and understand their current limitations.

## Set up inventory

1. Enable **Use Stockroom** in [feature settings](chapters/global-configuration/feature-settings.md#stockroom) as appropriate, then create the required stockrooms with their delivery settings. Review the default stockroom and blank-as-infinite setting before entering quantities.
2. Set product or variant quantities in the inventory view or product editor. Check warnings and reopen records to confirm saved quantities.
3. If stock images or notifications are required, configure them separately and verify their behavior with disposable products and safe test recipients.
4. Test storefront availability, preorder and checkout with an isolated shop. Back up stock data and review affected orders before resetting preorder counts or deleting a stockroom.
