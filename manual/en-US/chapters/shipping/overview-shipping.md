## Shipping

Shipping methods determine the choices available at checkout. redSHOP obtains methods from installed shipping plugins; the settings and whether they use rates or locations depend on each plugin.

- [Shipping Methods](chapters/shipping/shipping-methods.md)
- [Shipping Boxes](chapters/shipping/shipping-boxs.md)
- [Wrappings](chapters/shipping/wrappings.md)

## Set up and check shipping

1. Enable and configure an appropriate shipping plugin, then open its rate or location editor if it provides one.
2. Set the applicable destinations, product or customer conditions, price and tax options. Review copied rates carefully: not every setting is retained.
3. If the chosen workflow uses boxes, define their dimensions and priorities. Boxes do not guarantee that a mixed cart will physically fit.
4. Configure optional wrappings separately and check that the product template displays them.
5. Test matching and nonmatching destinations, rate boundaries, mixed carts and VAT with disposable data and mocked provider services before enabling live checkout.
