## Product order search

Open **Product Order Search** under Orders in redSHOP administration to inspect order-item records. Access is subject to administrator permissions; the results contain customer information.

<h2 id="overview">Results</h2>

The current list shows product name and SKU, order ID, the order item's modification date, order-item status, quantity and billing name. Product and order IDs link to their respective records. The displayed date comes from the order item, so do not assume it is the original order creation time.

<h2 id="filter">Filters</h2>

Search for and select a product, or filter by status and customer. Reset clears these selections. The product control selects a product ID rather than performing a persistent free-text search over all order-item names. Verify filtering and pagination with disposable orders.
