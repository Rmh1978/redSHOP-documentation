## Customization

Use these settings to organize product choices, extra information, destinations and redSHOP output:

- [Custom fields](chapters/customization/redshop.md) and [field groups](chapters/customization/custom.md) add and organize section-specific information.
- [Attribute sets](chapters/customization/attribute.md) group product choices and options.
- [Countries](chapters/customization/countries.md), [states](chapters/customization/states.md) and [postal codes](chapters/customization/zip-code.md) support address-related data.
- [Templates](chapters/customization/template.md) control section-specific redSHOP content.
- [Currencies](chapters/customization/currency.md) links to the currency and pricing guide.
- [Catalogues](chapters/customization/catalogues.md) manage catalogue choices and associated media.
- [Samples](chapters/customization/samples.md) manage sample choices and their colours or images.
- [Text Library](chapters/customization/text-library.md) stores reusable template content.
- [Customer tags](chapters/customization/customer-tags.md) associates customer labels with products.

The Customization menu also includes Sample Request. Availability and actions depend on the installation and administrator permissions.

Back up the shop before changing established records or templates. Test customer-facing effects on an isolated copy before applying changes to a live shop.
