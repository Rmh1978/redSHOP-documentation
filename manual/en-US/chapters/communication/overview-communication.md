## Communication

Manage transactional mail templates and newsletter content separately. Use safe test recipients and verify consent before sending.

- [Mails](chapters/communication/mails.md): transactional message templates.
- [Newsletters](chapters/communication/newletters.md): newsletter content and sending.
- [Newsletter subscribers](chapters/communication/newletters-subcribers.md): subscription records and consent considerations.

The **Communication** menu also offers **Newsletter Statistics**. Select a newsletter to see its recorded read and unread tracker counts; with no newsletter selected, the chart summarizes tracker counts by newsletter. The chart depends on Google Charts. Tracker counts are not proof that a message was delivered or read by a person.
