## Categories

Categories organize products into a hierarchy and control parts of their storefront presentation. See [Working with redSHOP categories](chapters/categories/redshop-categories.md) for the category list, editor, templates and product filters.

Start with a test category and choose its parent and category template. Assign
products, then check the hierarchy, publication state and storefront menu on an
isolated shop. The category guide also explains bulk template assignment and
the precautions for copying, deleting and rebuilding the tree.

For optional storefront navigation, see the [Category module](chapters/module-redshop/category-menu.md). Verify its documented limitations and direct access separately before use.
