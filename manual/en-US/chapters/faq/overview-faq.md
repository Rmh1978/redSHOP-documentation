## FAQ

Use these answers as a starting point for the installed redSHOP version. Verify
the exact extension package and its compatibility; an integration in the source
may not be installed or suitable for production. Rehearse payment and tax
changes on an isolated shop with disposable data and appropriate test services.

- [Is there a payment gateway for Cash On Delivery (COD)?](chapters/faq/payment.md)
- [Using PayPal with redSHOP](chapters/faq/paypal.md)
- [How do I customize the AJAX add-to-cart boxes?](chapters/faq/how-do-1.md)
- [How do I configure a shop where VAT is not applicable?](chapters/faq/how-do-2.md)

The payment answers distinguish configuration from verified payment receipt.
The tax answer does not replace advice about the shop's legal obligations.
