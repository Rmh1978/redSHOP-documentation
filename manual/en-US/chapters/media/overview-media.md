## Media

Manage media records and their associations with products, categories and other shop items. The guide covers the media list, product-specific media and precautions for uploads and deletion.

- [Working With Medias in redSHOP](chapters/media/working-medias.md)
- [Product Files and protected downloads](chapters/product-management/products-redshop.md)

Media records and their associated files can be affected by different upload
and deletion paths. Back up files as well as the database and rehearse changes
on an isolated copy. Public product media is not a substitute for protected
downloads; verify the separate Product Files workflow and access rules before
offering files to customers.
